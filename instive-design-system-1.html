06

Voice & tone

Talk like an operator on the floor, not a vendor in a boardroom. Concrete, fast, never breathless.

◆ We sound like
  • "Quote it before they ask twice."
  • Specific numbers, real lanes, real SKUs
  • Plain verbs: route, price, track, clear
  • Confident about outcomes, honest about limits
  • Short. Operators are busy.
✕ We never sound like
  • "Revolutionary AI-powered synergy"
  • Vague "solutions" with no number
  • Hype, exclamation stacks, jargon soup
  • Overpromising autonomy we can't ship
  • Long paragraphs nobody reads
07

Spacing & radius

An 8px base keeps everything on the freight grid. Radius stays tight — 4px — because logistics is engineered, not soft.
